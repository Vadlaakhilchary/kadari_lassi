Our Story

A trusted local favourite for over 30 years, Kadari's Lassi Since 1991 serves fresh and traditional lassi made with quality ingredients. We are renowned for making the best lassi in Hyderabad, following the same recipe and pouring the same love since 1991.

We believe that good food comes from honest hands. Every glass of our famous thick lassi is a promise of purity, freshness, and tradition. For anyone looking for great lassi in Malkajgiri, our shop is a landmark of taste.
