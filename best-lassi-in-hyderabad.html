Our Legacy

Why Kadari's is the Best Lassi in Hyderabad

For over three decades, Kadari's Lassi has been a household name in Malkajgiri, famous for serving the most authentic and thick lassi in Hyderabad. Our journey is one of passion, purity, and tradition.

A Story Since 1991

Our story began in a small shop with a simple mission: to serve the freshest, most delicious lassi using a secret family recipe. We believe in quality over everything, which is why generations of families trust us for that authentic taste. When you search for the 'best lassi near me' in Hyderabad, you'll find a legacy of flavor at Kadari's.

What Makes Our Lassi Famous?

It's the commitment to tradition. We use only the purest milk and curd, with no artificial additives. Our lassi is known for being incredibly thick and creamy, a quality that sets us apart. This dedication is why we are considered the home of the best thick lassi Hyderabad has to offer.
